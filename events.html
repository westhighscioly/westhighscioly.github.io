Our Events

23 events across five categories. Every member competes in 2–3, paired with a teammate for most of the season.

Official 2026–27 Division C events, verified against the current list at soinc.org. Full rules for each event are published there too.
Category 1

Life, Personal & Social Science

Events that dig into biology, medicine, and the human impact on ecosystems — mostly test-based with image and specimen identification.

Anatomy & Physiology

In-depth study of human body systems — this year's rotation typically covers 2–3 systems in detail.

Botany

Plant biology, physiology, and identification — from cellular structure to whole-plant classification.

Designer Genes

Genetics and biotechnology, from Mendelian inheritance to modern gene-editing techniques.

Disease Detectives

Epidemiology puzzle-solving: trace outbreaks, read study designs, and calculate risk statistics.

Water Quality

Aquatic ecosystems and water testing — chemical, biological, and physical indicators of water health.

Category 2

Earth & Space Science

From the rock cycle to distant galaxies — these events reward students who love field science and astronomy.

Astronomy

Stellar evolution, deep-sky objects, and telescope data analysis using tools like DS9.

Dynamic Planet

Rotating topic on Earth systems — glaciers, oceanography, or space weather, depending on the season.

Remote Sensing

Interpret satellite and aerial imagery to analyze Earth's surface, weather, and land use.

Rocks & Minerals

Mineral properties, rock classification, and identification using real hand samples.

Category 3

Physical Science & Chemistry

Lab-based events that mix conceptual physics and chemistry with hands-on measurement and problem-solving.

Chemistry Lab

Stoichiometry, titrations, and thermochemistry problems solved live at the lab bench.

Circuit Lab

Circuit analysis and troubleshooting — building and diagnosing analog circuits under time pressure.

Forensics

Analyze simulated crime-scene evidence using chemistry, chromatography, and trace analysis.

Hovercraft

Design and build a hovercraft, then tune it to complete a precision course.

Protein Modeling

Build physical models of protein structures to understand molecular biology and biochemistry.

Thermodynamics

Heat, energy transfer, and thermal physics problems, from engines to everyday systems.

Category 4

Technology & Engineering

Our build events — students design, test, and iterate devices for months before bringing them to competition.

Boomilever

Design a wood cantilevered structure to hold maximum load at minimum weight.

Electric Vehicle

Engineer a battery-powered vehicle that stops as close as possible to a target distance.

Mission Possible

Build a Rube Goldberg-style device that completes a chain of tasks within a time window.

Wright Stuff

Design and fly a rubber-band powered balsa aircraft for maximum time aloft.

Category 5

Inquiry & Nature of Science

Events that test scientific reasoning and problem-solving skills that cut across every discipline.

Codebusters

Cryptography event covering classical and modern ciphers, solved by hand against the clock.

Engineering CAD

Technical drawing and computer-aided design — read and produce precise engineering drawings.

Experimental Design

Design, run, and analyze a real experiment from a mystery prompt handed out on the spot.

Ping-Pong Parachute

Build a drop device that keeps a ping-pong ball airborne as long as possible.
