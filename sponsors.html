Why Sponsor Us

What sponsorships fund

  • Tournament registration fees — entry costs for every invitational, regional, and state tournament we attend.

  • Build materials — balsa wood, motors, circuit components, and other supplies for our engineering events.

  • Travel — transportation to tournaments across Southern California, from Temecula to San Diego.

  • Team gear — team shirts and supplies that help us travel and compete as one team.
