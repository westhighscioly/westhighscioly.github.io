About Our Team

Who we are, what we stand for, and the people who make it happen.

Our Mission

Making rigorous science fun, collaborative, and open to everyone

The West High School Science Olympiad team gives students in West Torrance a place to go deep on science and engineering outside the classroom. We field a roster of 35 students each year, competing across all 23 Science Olympiad events, from written knowledge tests to physical builds tested on competition day.

We believe you don't need to be a straight-A student to belong here — you need curiosity and a willingness to put in the study hours.

35 students. 23 events across 5 categories, all season long.

What we value

  • Deep learning over memorization
  • Mentorship between upperclassmen and new members
  • Iteration — our best builds go through many prototypes
  • Sportsmanship at every tournament we attend
Our Story

Team history

Nobody wrote down how the program got started — but tournament archives put our first result all the way back in 1996. 49 tournaments and counting since then.

1996

Where our records begin

The earliest tournament appearance we could find in the archives — the start of a program that's now competed 49 times over. If you know the earlier story, we'd love to add it here.

2016 & 2017

Best Regional finish: 1st place

Our best-ever Regional result — back-to-back 1st place finishes two years running.

2017

Best season on record

On top of winning Regionals that year, the team also posted its best invitational finish ever (3rd place) and tied its best-ever State finish at 10th — a mark also hit in 2006 and 2021.

2018 & 2025

Strong Regional showings: 4th place

Two more solid Regional finishes, seven years apart — proof the team has been consistently competitive for a long time.

2025–2026

State qualifier, 19 medals

Qualified for State by placing 6th of 24 at the LA Regional, then went on to earn 19 medals across the season, including 1st place in Codebusters. Full breakdown on the Schedule & Results page.

2026–2027

Current season

This season, we're fielding a full roster across all five event categories and building toward our next Regional. See the Schedule & Results page for details.
